# Bishop Snipe: anime VFX (2026-10-19)

**The user's brief:** add VFX to the Snipe cutscene in the anime style of the references in
[[Design/Cutscene Storyboards]]: Chromatic Opus, Enuma Elish, and Develuper's VFX guide. This
follows [[Changelog/2026-10-19 Snipe cutscene rebuild]].

## Textures (`art/vfx/make_textures.py`)
- The script draws 8 textures, white on a transparent background, so the game can tint them:

  | Texture | Use |
  |---|---|
  | `glow` | soft round glow |
  | `spark` | streak |
  | `star` | impact star |
  | `ring` | shockwave ring |
  | `speedlines` | radial speed lines |
  | `crack` | ground crack |
  | `energy` | beam texture that tiles along its length |
  | `slash` | crescent slash |

- They were uploaded with the Studio MCP's `upload_image` (served from a local http.server), so
  they're owned by kaydnn2043. Their ids are in `VfxKit.T`.
- All 8 load in a playtest. The first check showed 4 failing; they loaded a few minutes later, so
  they were probably still in moderation.

## `VfxKit.lua` (new, client)
All world effects go in the cutscene's folder and clean themselves up.

| Function | What it makes | Who sees it |
|---|---|---|
| `shockwave` | an expanding ring, flat or standing up | everyone nearby |
| `decal` | a fading flat image (cracks) | everyone nearby |
| `flare` | a camera-facing star or glow that grows and spins | everyone nearby |
| `sparks` | a burst of streak particles | everyone nearby |
| `charge` | particles drawn inwards, plus a growing glow and light | everyone nearby |
| `beam` | an energy beam with a scrolling texture | everyone nearby |
| `aura` | an outline only (a fill tinted white pieces pink), plus a light | everyone nearby |
| `impactFrame` | a ColorCorrection on Lighting: a blown-out white frame, then a stark red-and-black frame | the seated player only |
| `speedLines` | redrawn at a random angle each frame, hand-drawn style | the seated player only |
| `bloom` | extra glow while the cutscene plays | the seated player only |

## The scenes
| Scene | VFX added |
|---|---|
| 1 Load | Bloom on. A red outline on the bishop. Red energy streams into the bishop (a charge). When the rifle lands: a red shockwave on the board, a white-hot star, red sparks, and a red outline on the rifle. Gold sparks as the bolt throws the shell. |
| 2 Scope | A red lock-on ring spins and closes in on the target, then pings with a star. A glint on the scope lens. Spectators see both. |
| 3 Fire | A red charge at the muzzle, then **the impact frame** (white-hot, then red and black) with speed lines and a hitstop before the recoil. Muzzle: a big star, a red glow, two shockwave rings along the barrel, and a cone of sparks. |
| 4 Hit | The bullet glows red and leaves a red energy beam behind it, which fades at the end. **The first hit** gets a red impact frame. Every hit: a star, a glow, a red and a white ring on the board, a ring across the bullet's path, red sparks, a crack on the square, splinters, and a 0.07 s hitstop. **The king:** gold sparks thrown back and up, a gold star, a white slash crescent and a gold ring. At the end: dust on every hit square. |

## Tested (Studio playtest)
Position `8/8/7q/6r1/5k2/4n3/8/K1B5 w`, Bc1 snipes NE. Checked frames:
- the outline on the load shot;
- the lock-on ring in the scope view;
- the white impact frame with speed lines on the shot;
- the red impact frame on the first hit;
- the star and sparks on the hits;
- the cracks and the energy line at the end.

No errors, and no ColorCorrection or Bloom left in Lighting afterwards.

Fixed during testing: the outline's fill tinted the white bishop pink, and the lock-on ring washed
the scope view pink.
