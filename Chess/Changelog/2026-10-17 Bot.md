# Phase 3: bot (2026-10-17)

- **`Shared/Bot/Search`** is pure Luau:
  - evaluation: material plus Michniewski piece-square tables, with the king tables blended
    middlegame → endgame;
  - negamax alpha-beta with iterative deepening and a check extension;
  - quiescence on captures and queen promotions (6 plies);
  - MVV-LVA ordering, with the previous best move first;
  - a root repetition check, so it doesn't walk into threefold;
  - a time budget, yielding every ~12 ms so the server keeps running (worst frame measured:
    22 ms).
- **Levels** (`Search.LEVELS`):

  | Level | Depth | Budget | Picks among moves within | Random move chance | Min think |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Beginner | 1 | – | 150 cp | 20% | 0.8 s |
  | Casual | 2 | – | 50 cp | 5% | 0.8 s |
  | Club | 3 | – | 15 cp | – | 0.6 s |
  | Strong | 6, iterative deepening | 2.5 s | best move | – | 0.4 s |

  - The weaker levels never pass up a found mate.
  - Strong reaches about depth 5 in the opening and depth 4 in Kiwipete, at 20–100k nodes/s.
- **Abilities-ready:** the bot searches `Moves.legal` plus `mode.abilityActions`, through the new
  `Game:push` / `Game:pop`. It works on `Game:clone()`, so the live game never changes mid-search.
- **Tables** (`ChessTable`):
  - a seated player can invite a bot (`Lobby "addBot", level`) or remove it (`"removeBot"`).
    `"botLevels"` lists the levels;
  - bots are always ready, move on their own, and leave when no human is left;
  - on a clock, the bot spends at most `left/30 + increment/2`;
  - a search that finishes after a resign, game end or new game is dropped;
  - bot games go into the `<Mode>_bot` stats bucket.
- **Tests**, all passing:
  - `Shared/Tests/BotTests` (Edit mode): mate in 1, winning a hanging queen, promotion,
    defending against a back-rank mate, and leaving the searched position unchanged.
    `.speed()` reports depth and nodes;
  - in a playtest via ChessDebug (new verbs: `addBot`, `playerAddBot`, `removeBot`, `start`,
    `botSpeed`):
    - human vs bot replies, with no hitch;
    - resigning mid-think drops the bot's move;
    - the bot leaves an empty table;
    - a bot as White opens on a 3+2 clock;
    - bad levels are refused;
    - bot vs bot at every level runs to checkmate: L2 beat L1, L3 beat L2, L4 beat L3, and L4 beat L1
      in 29 plies.
- Seen in the console: `Remotes.State` "queue exhausted", because no client listens yet. Phase 4's
  client fixes it.
- Next: phase 4, the board, the room and input.
